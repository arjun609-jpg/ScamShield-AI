    About ScamShield AI

    Protect before you pay. Paste a suspicious message or link and see why it may be risky.

    • This prototype uses transparent, rule-based checks. It is not a trained AI model.
    • It does not connect to banks, verify senders or open links.
    • The score shows how many warning signs matched. It is not the chance that something is a fraud.
